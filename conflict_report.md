## Reading

Even in this partial tranche the RQ4 hypothesis pattern is visible:
the conflict graph's central figures are the *accused bridges* —
Aziru (accused from Byblos, Tyre, and implicated at Damascus) and the
Milkilu/Tagi/sons-of-Labaya bloc — not the loudest accusers. The
inter-vassal politics invisible in the correspondence layer (which
has literally zero vassal-vassal edges beyond two) is the dominant
structure here. Full coverage and second coding in tranche 2.
